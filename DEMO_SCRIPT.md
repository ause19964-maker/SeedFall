# SEEDFALL — 6-minute demonstration

Everything shown uses the **demonstration workspace**: a separate database of fictional records. Before presenting: Settings → General → *Recreate demonstration data* (if it exists), set an operator name such as “Presenter”, open the demonstration, then Alerts & Tasks → **Check now** once.

**0:00 — The honest starting point (40 s)**
On a fresh install (or describe it): *“Nothing is recorded yet.”* No invented varieties, no zero-filled charts — three ways in (add your first seed, import a catalogue, learn how evidence works) and an explicit option to look at fictional data. Open the demonstration; point out the striped banner: every record here is fictional and in a separate file.

**0:40 — Home answers three questions (50 s)**
*What seeds do we have?* — counts and recorded stock from stored records only, and the holdings bar: 3 of 6 varieties are held by one custodian. *What needs attention?* — open alerts by severity. *What should I do next?* — due tasks and the next step of each urgent alert. The **Add seed** button and the bell are in the same place on every page.

**1:30 — An alert, explained (60 s)**
Click the bell (unread count) → *Moonstripe Lablab Bean: no independent backup is recorded in this workspace*. Expand *Why, evidence and notification history*: the evidence (one custodian), the rule, the uncertainty (*seed may exist elsewhere without being recorded here*), and the in-app notification. Show a **retest** alert: it says *a retest falling due does not mean the seed has declined*. Acknowledge one; resolve another with a written note — it is attributed and never re-raised for the same event. Mention: checks run every hour inside the server even with no browser open, and `RUN_ALERT_CHECK.bat` covers the times SEEDFALL is closed.

**2:30 — Last Seed Guardian (90 s)**
Preservation Plans → **Last Seed Guardian** → Moonstripe Lablab Bean.
- Step 2: the actual evidence — DEMO-BEAN-A 640 g, tested 74.0% on 20 May 2025 (*historical*); DEMO-BEAN-B has no test — *performance unknown, not assumed poor*.
- Step 3: rule-based risks, each with evidence, rule and uncertainty; labelled *not an AI prediction*.
- Step 4: suggestions — verify stock, inspect storage, schedule a germination test, identify a backup source. Untick one, give two an owner.
- Step 5: **Create plan** → the plan page with tasks and the saved review.
- Step 6: back in the Guardian, imagine DEMO-BEAN-A and DEMO-BEAN-B lost → **Preview this loss**: holdings 1 → 0, *Records unchanged*.

**4:00 — Doing the work (40 s)**
On the plan, **Mark done…** a task: it requires a written outcome and records who did it. Point out: no plan, task, alert or simulation ever changes stock — only a completed distribution or a logged correction does.

**4:40 — Seed passport (40 s)**
Open DEMO-BEAN-A → **Create seed passport** → the QR code; **Open passport**: identifier, variety, origin, quantity, latest valid test, storage history, open alerts — and no custodian, location or names. **Print label** shows the packet label. Be explicit: the code opens only where SEEDFALL can be reached (this computer by default), because SEEDFALL has no login.

**5:20 — The full simulator and close (40 s)**
Preservation Plans → **Scenario simulator** for the five-step Last Seed Protocol with interventions (e.g. a 200 g safety duplicate to Riverbend): solid panels are recorded, hatched panels hypothetical.
Close: SEEDFALL shows what is recorded, what the evidence does and doesn't say, what needs attention, and what to do next — with every number traceable. Limits: only what is recorded; thresholds are configurable heuristics; records cannot prove genetic diversity or that seed doesn't exist elsewhere. **Leave demonstration** — the operational workspace is exactly as it was.
